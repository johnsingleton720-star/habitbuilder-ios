HabitBuilder.pro is a web-based app wrapped in a Capacitor WebView for native iOS distribution.

The app requires an internet connection. Users sign in via third-party authentication (OpenID Connect) which opens a brief browser window for login — no username/password is needed.

To test the app:
1. Open the app and tap "Get Started"
2. Create an account through the sign-in flow
3. The onboarding wizard will guide you to create your first habit
4. The AI will generate a personalized action plan
5. Explore the dashboard, track habits, and view progress

In-App Purchases: The app offers free tier functionality (1 habit, 3 sessions/week). Pro and Premium subscriptions unlock additional features. A "Restore Purchases" button is available in Settings.

The app uses OpenAI for AI-powered coaching via API. No user data is stored by OpenAI.
