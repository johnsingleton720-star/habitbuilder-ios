General Best Practices for Most Apps (Especially Subscription/Utility Apps Like Yours)
For apps where the core value is personalization (AI coach, habit plans, trackers), the winning pattern in 2026 is:
•  Anonymous/value-first onboarding (what you already have): Let users experience the magic (plan building, preview) without signing up. This boosts completion rates dramatically because users invest time and emotion before being asked for anything.
•  Immediate trial prompt at the “aha” moment: Right after the personalized plan preview is the highest-conversion moment. Users have just seen something useful and unique — that’s when they’re most likely to say yes to a 7-day trial.
•  One-tap auth as the trial gate: Make signing up feel like the enabler of the value they just saw, not a separate hurdle. Apple Sign In first (which you already implemented) is the gold standard here.
•  Minimal or progressive post-sign-up onboarding: Any remaining setup happens after the trial starts, when users are already committed and invested.
Why this beats a traditional long onboarding:
•  Your data shows the long pre-sign-up onboarding gets people 70–80% of the way but then drops them at auth/trial. 2026 data shows that once users hit friction after investing time, they default to “free plan” or close the app (Day-0 cancellations are the #1 reason trials fail).
•  Benchmarks: Apps that reach the “aha” in the first 60–90 seconds see 2–5× higher trial-start rates than those with heavy upfront onboarding. Hard paywalls or trial prompts after value convert 5× better than pure freemium in many categories, but only if the value is shown first.
