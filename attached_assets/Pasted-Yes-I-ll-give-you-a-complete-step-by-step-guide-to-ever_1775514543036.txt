1. Custom Product Page (CPP) — What It Is and Why It Helps You
A Custom Product Page is an Apple feature that lets you create one or more alternative versions of your main App Store product page. You can change the screenshots, preview video, and promotional text while keeping the same app metadata (description, keywords, price, etc.).
How it helps your specific situation:
•  Your ad traffic is cold and comparison-shopping. A standard App Store page forces users to scroll through generic screenshots before seeing the 7-day trial and AI plan value.
•  A CPP lets you create a tailored landing page for ad users that leads immediately with the personalized plan preview + clear “Start 7-Day Free Trial” CTA. This shortens the path to the trial decision and can lift tap-to-sign-up/trial rates by 15–40% (2026 AppTweak & SplitMetrics benchmarks for subscription apps).
•  You can link ads directly to the CPP instead of the main page, so every dollar you spend on Apple Search Ads goes to a higher-converting experience.
•  It costs nothing and takes ~30–60 minutes to set up.
Exact Step-by-Step Guide to Create and Use a CPP for HabitBuilder.pro
1.  Log into App Store Connect Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com → select your app → App Store tab → Product Pages (left sidebar).
2.  Create a new Custom Product Page
	•  Click the + button next to “Custom Product Pages”.
	•  Choose iPhone (or iPad if you support it) as the device type.
	•  Give it a clear internal name, e.g., “Ad Traffic – AI Plan + Trial”.
3.  Design the CPP (this is where you make it convert)
	•  First screenshot: Use the exact “Your 7-Day [Goal] Plan” preview screen from your app (the one users see after the quick setup). Overlay text: “Your Personalized AI Habit Plan is Ready” + “Start 7-Day Free Trial – No Card Needed”.
	•  Second screenshot: Show the plan preview with the unlock bullets we discussed (AI coaching, streaks, guided sessions, etc.).
	•  Third screenshot: Highlight the trial benefits + “Cancel anytime before day 8”.
	•  Preview video (optional but powerful): 15–30 second screen recording of a user building a plan and starting the trial.
	•  Promotional text (top of the page): “Get Your Custom AI Habit Plan in 30 Seconds + 7-Day Free Trial”.
4.  Save and submit for review
	•  Apple reviews CPPs quickly (usually 1–24 hours). Once approved, it gets its own unique URL.
5.  Link it to your ads (critical step)
	•  Go back to Apple Ads dashboard → your campaign → Ad Groups → edit the ad group.
	•  Under “Destination”, change from the default App Store page to your new CPP URL.
	•  Do this for both the Basic and Advanced campaigns so every ad click goes to the optimized page.
6.  Test it
	•  Use a test Apple ID on a real device, search for your app, tap the ad, and confirm you land on the CPP.
	•  Track performance in App Store Connect Analytics → Acquisition → Custom Product Pages to see which version converts best.
Do this today — it’s the single highest-ROI change you can make with zero extra spend.
