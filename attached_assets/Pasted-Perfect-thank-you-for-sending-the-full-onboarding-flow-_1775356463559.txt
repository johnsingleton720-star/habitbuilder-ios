Step-by-Step Flow Critique (What’s Working + Where Users Drop)
I numbered the screens in the exact order you sent them:
1.  Landing / Welcome screen (“Build habits that actually stick”)
	•  Strength: Strong headline, clear 30-second promise, 7-day trial mentioned early, big green CTA.
	•  Issue: The “Already have an account? Sign in” link is easy to miss. New ad users rarely tap it.
2.  “What brings you here?” (multi-choice)
	•  Strength: Personalization starts immediately — great for AI relevance.
	•  Issue: None major, but this is still pre-account.
3.  “Pick your first habit” (grid + custom)
	•  Strength: Visual and low-friction.
	•  Issue: None.
4.  “How would you like to track?” (AI Coaching vs Simple Tracking)
	•  Strength: “Recommended” badge on AI plan is smart.
	•  Issue: Still no mention of trial or account — users think they’re getting this for free.
5–6. “Quick setup” + “Building your plan…”
•  Strength: Progress bar + loading steps feel premium.
•  Issue: The plan is fully built before any sign-up. Users now have emotional investment in “their” plan but haven’t been asked for any commitment.
7.  “Your 7-Day Evening Exercise Plan” preview
	•  Strength: Beautiful reveal.
	•  Issue: This is the first time the trial is prominently pushed — after the plan exists. Too late.
8–11. “Create your account” + Replit/Google/Apple flows
•  Major friction point: Account creation is required after the plan is built. Replit auth adds an external browser redirect + permission screen. Cold users hate this. Many drop here (explains why “many drop off” before finishing).
12–13. Trial selection screen + “Continue without a trial?” modal
•  Critical drop-off: You circled the exact spots. “No thanks, continue with the free plan” is large, low-friction, and feels like the default. The confirmation modal reinforces skipping.
•  Nuance: For non-US users (you mentioned possible Russian traffic), $6–$15/mo feels expensive even with a trial — they’ll grab the free plan instantly.
14.  Final “Welcome” screen
•  Strength: Friendly.
•  Issue: The trial is now optional and buried. Users who chose “No thanks” stay in free tier forever unless nudged later.
Overall implications: Users complete the fun/engaging part (plan building) first, then hit the “paywall” moment when they’re already tired or skeptical. Ad-driven users (colder traffic) are especially likely to bail at account creation or the trial screen. This matches your observation: “Many drop off… For all the downloads I don’t see people going through the onboarding all the way.”
Why This Explains Zero Subscriptions Tab Data
Apple’s March 25, 2026 Analytics only records a “Trial Started” event when a user explicitly accepts the 7-day offer and the StoreKit purchase flow begins. Right now, most users never reach (or choose) that moment. Once we move the trial earlier and make skipping harder, the tab will start populating within 24–48 hours of the next big install wave.
